# Governance Model: Agila Manifesto v3.1

The **Agila Manifesto** is an open socio-technical framework designed for human and machine collaboration. Because its foundation relies on theoretical invariants, systems theory, and reproducible signals rather than marketing consensus, this repository establishes a clear **3-tier governance structure** to ensure integrity, linguistic neutrality, and open empirical adaptation.

---

## 1. Non-Negotiable Core Tenet: Human Sovereignty

1. **Machines Never Modify Canon**:
   - AI agents, LLMs, and automated tools may read, parse, index, cite, and generate analytical proposals.
   - **No automated system or AI agent has authority to modify, approve, or merge changes into the canon (`canon/`).**
   - Any pull request modifying `canon/` that is created or approved without verifiable human signoff will be rejected by CI guards.
2. **Mandatory Human Ownership**:
   - All proposals touching the canon or generated by agents must declare an active human sponsor via `Human-owner: @username`.
3. **Descriptive, Non-Dogmatic Framing**:
   - Governance bodies and stewards must never treat the manifesto principles as mandatory certifications, rigid compliance checklists, or performance evaluation rubrics for teams or individuals.

## 3. Tier 1: Fundamento (`canon/`)

The canon represents the sovereign, canonical anchor of the Agila Manifesto in Esperanto. Its protection is deliberately rigorous ("celosa") to prevent erratic churn, cultural drift, or dogmatic capture.

### Requirements for Canon Modification Proposals
Every proposal to evolve, add, modify, or retire a canon principle must be submitted as a formal RFC issue (via `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/01_canon_propono.yml`) and include:
1. **Verbatim Text**: The proposed Esperanto text.
2. **Theoretical Base (`Bazo`)**: The underlying scientific, mathematical, or empirical grounding (e.g. Graph Theory, Shannon Channel Capacity, Simon's Nearly Decomposable Systems, Little's Law, Lehman's Laws).
3. **Base Grounding Status**: Explicit classification as:
   - `Teorema` (Theorem): Mathematically or formally proven.
   - `Modelo` (Model): Established theoretical formulation with structural validity.
   - `Tendencia empírica` (Empirical Tendency): Consistently observed empirical pattern.
   - `Interpretación` (Interpretation): Pragmatic socio-technical lens.
4. **Observable Signal (`Signalo`)**: Concrete, falsifiable symptoms observable in real systems when the structural principle is violated.
5. **Counterexample Analysis**: A rigorous assessment of real-world scenarios or boundary conditions where the principle may fail, produce perverse incentives, or require qualification.
6. **Human Owner**: A designated human sponsor (`Human-owner: @username`).

### Approval and Deliberation Rules
- **Minimum 14 Days Deliberation**: A proposal must remain open for public deliberation for at least 14 full calendar days. No pull request touching `canon/` can be merged before this window elapses.
- **Approval Threshold**: Must receive explicit, recorded approval from **at least 2 human stewards** (`stewards`).
- **Zero Open Counterexamples**: If an unresolved, empirically substantiated counterexample is open against the proposal, the proposal cannot be approved until the principle wording or boundaries are adapted to address it.
- **Single Steward Fallback Rule**:
  - If due to emeritus transition or temporary unavailability only 1 human steward is active, that steward **cannot unilaterally alter the canon**.
  - To proceed with an emergency or critical update, the sole steward must publish a formal justification in the repository discussion/issue log and initiate an active, public onboarding process to recruit and verify at least 2 additional human stewards.

## 4. Tier 2: Tradukoj (`skills/`, `i18n/`)

Translations and machine-readable agent skills enable international adoption and AI agent collaboration. Because the Esperanto canon is the sole authoritative source of truth, translations are explicitly derivative.

### Translation Maintenance Rules
- **Maintainer + Native Reviewer**: Approving a new translation or substantial revision requires approval from the designated language maintainer plus at least 1 native speaker reviewer.
- **Metadata Alignment**:
  - Each skill file (`skills/agila-manifesto-<lang>/SKILL.md`) must specify `metadata.canon_version` and `metadata.canon_sha256`.
  - Automated CI compares the declared SHA-256 against `canon/AGILA-MANIFESTO.eo.md`. If canon evolves, the skill is flagged as `stale` until updated.
- **Principle Invariants**:
  - All 10 principles must be present in sequence, preserving canonical identifiers `P01` through `P10`.
  - Must include the mandatory derivative disclaimer acknowledging that the Esperanto canon prevails in any discrepancy.
  - Must preserve the universal agent protocol: **read**, **analyze**, **cite**, and **propose** without self-approval authority.

## 5. Tier 3: Uzado (`uzado/`)

The `uzado/` layer is the open laboratory where practitioners share real-world implementations, organizational experiments, case studies, and anti-patterns.

### Uzado Contribution Rules
- **Low Protection Barrier**: Requires only 1 approving review from any maintainer or designated community reviewer.
- **Freedom of Expression**: Authors may share domain-specific interpretations, industry contexts, and empirical evidence.
- **Strictly Non-Commercial**: Submissions must not serve as advertisements, promotional vehicles for consulting services, or product placements.
- **Welcome Counterexamples**: Submissions demonstrating where principles break down ("a counterexample is a gift") are given equal prominence alongside success stories.

## 6. Semantic Versioning for Canon (SemVer)

The canon adheres strictly to Semantic Versioning (`MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`):

### MAJOR (`vX.0.0`)
A MAJOR bump occurs when the fundamental structure of the canon changes:
- Adding a new principle (e.g. expanding beyond 10 principles).
- Removing an existing principle.
- Altering the fundamental semantic direction, core invariant, or intent of an existing principle.

### MINOR (`v3.X.0`)
A MINOR bump occurs when the grounding or framing evolves while preserving the core invariant:
- Modifying, refining, or replacing the theoretical base (`Bazo`) of a principle.
- Modifying, refining, or replacing the observable signal (`Signalo`) of a principle.
- Substantively altering or expanding the canonical introductory framing, definitions, or concluding declarations.

### PATCH (`v3.1.X`)
A PATCH bump occurs when editorial or typographical refinements are made:
- Orthographic, grammatical, or typographical corrections in the Esperanto text.
- Wording improvements that enhance clarity without shifting the underlying semantic meaning or theoretical base.
