# Principle 1: Every System Mirrors the Communication Structure of Its Creators

> **Canon (Esperanto)**:  
> *1. Ĉiu sistemo spegulas la komunikan strukturon de siaj kreintoj.  
> Organizo kaj sistemo estas du vidoj de unu strukturo. Elekti la teaman strukturon do estas elekti arkitekturon, kaj strukturo, kiun oni ne elektas, formiĝas hazarde.  
> Bazo: ekzistas proksimuma strukturkonserva bildigo, homomorfio, de la komunika grafo al la modula grafo de la sistemo; la inversa manovro elektas la fonton por ricevi la celon.  
> Signalo: la dezirata arkitekturo ne kongruas kun la teamoj; malgranda ŝanĝo postulas kunordigi multajn teamojn.*

---

## English Translation
Organization and system are two views of one structure. Choosing the team structure is therefore choosing an architecture, and a structure one does not choose forms by chance.

- **Basis**: There exists an approximate structure-preserving mapping, a homomorphism, from the communication graph to the module graph of the system; the inverse maneuver chooses the source to achieve the target.
- **Signal**: The desired architecture does not match the teams; a small change requires coordinating many teams.

---

## Theoretical Grounding: Conway's Law and Graph Homomorphism

Melvin Conway formulated in 1968 that organizations design systems that copy their internal communication structures. In modern graph theory, this constraint can be formalized as an approximate homomorphism $\phi$ between the communication graph of the organization $G_C = (V_C, E_C)$ and the module graph of the software architecture $G_M = (V_M, E_M)$.

When an edge $(u, v) \in E_C$ exists (i.e., team $u$ and team $v$ communicate frequently), their software components $\phi(u)$ and $\phi(v)$ naturally develop coupling. Conversely, if two teams do not have a strong, low-latency communication channel, their software components cannot maintain tight synchronization without immense friction.

### The Inverse Conway Maneuver
Recognizing that organizational communication is the independent variable and architecture is the dependent variable, engineering organizations execute the **Inverse Conway Maneuver**:
1. Identify the desired modular software architecture based on domain boundaries (e.g., Domain-Driven Design bounded contexts).
2. Organize teams and their communication channels to mirror that modular architecture.
3. Decouple cross-team dependencies so teams can deliver value autonomously without synchronous coordination meetings.

---

## Operational Diagnostic: The Signal in Practice

- **Observable Signal**: A feature change that should logically be isolated to a single business capability requires synchronized pull requests, release trains, and coordination meetings across three or four separate teams (e.g., DBA team, API team, UI team, Platform team).
- **Structural Remedy**: Realign horizontal functional silos into cross-functional, stream-aligned squads that own their capabilities from end to end.
