# Principle 3: Stable Systems are Strong Inside and Weak Across Boundaries

> **Canon (Esperanto)**:  
> *3. Stabilaj sistemoj estas fortaj ene kaj malfortaj trans la limoj.  
> Ene de unuo la komunikado estas rapida, dudirekta kaj kun komuna kunteksto, kaj la decidoj okazas tie, kie troviĝas la informo. Inter unuoj la ligo fariĝas pakto, kiam ĝi havas kvar ecojn: ĉiuj flankoj konsentis, nomitaj respondeculoj devontigis sin, la historio estas spurebla, kaj iu ajn povas kontroli la plenumon. La ilo, la formato kaj la papero ne gravas; gravas la ecoj. En sana sistemo la nombro de paktoj kaj kunsidoj estas proporcia al la dependeco.  
> Bazo: kompleksaj sistemoj estas preskaŭ dekomponeblaj, kun interagoj pli fortaj ene de subsistemoj ol inter ili; reguliganto bezonas variecon almenaŭ egalan al tiu de la reguligata sistemo, do distribuita decido akordas kun distribuita informo; interfacoj kunmeteblas nur kiam iliaj tipoj kongruas.  
> Signalo: kunsidoj nur por kunordigi ŝanĝojn; paneoj ĉe transdono inter teamoj.*

---

## English Translation
Within a unit, communication is fast, bidirectional, and with shared context, and decisions occur where the information resides. Between units, a connection becomes a pact when it has four properties: all parties agreed, named owners committed, history is traceable, and anyone can verify fulfillment. Tooling, format, and paper do not matter; the properties matter. In a healthy system, the number of pacts and meetings is proportional to dependency.

- **Basis**: Complex systems are nearly decomposable, with interactions stronger within subsystems than between them; a regulator requires variety at least equal to that of the system being regulated, so distributed decision-making aligns with distributed information; interfaces compose only when their types match.
- **Signal**: Meetings held solely to coordinate changes; failures during handoffs between teams.

---

## Theoretical Grounding: Nearly Decomposable Systems and Boundary Pacts

1. **Herbert Simon's Nearly Decomposable Systems**: Complex adaptive systems that endure exhibit hierarchy and near-decomposability: intra-component interactions are high-frequency and intense, while inter-component interactions are low-frequency, narrow, and formal. If cross-boundary interactions are as frequent as internal communications, the boundary is artificial and stability is lost.
2. **Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety**: "Only variety can absorb variety." Centralized management cannot process the exponential variety of localized domain issues. Decentralizing decisions to the point where information originates matches regulatory capacity to system variety.
3. **The 4 Properties of a Boundary Pact**:
   - **Mutual Agreement**: Reached by consensus among all participating teams.
   - **Named Commitments**: Accountable human owners are publicly declared.
   - **Traceable History**: Auditable evolution tracked in version control.
   - **Verifiable Fulfillment**: Independent programmatic or mechanical verification.

---

## Operational Diagnostic: The Signal in Practice

- **Observable Signal**: Calendar overload filled with multi-team alignment meetings simply to coordinate schema updates or deployment sequences; constant regression incidents at boundary handoffs.
- **Structural Remedy**: Eliminate the coordinating committees. Harden boundaries by replacing informal handshakes with explicit, consumer-driven contract tests and automated verification pipelines.
